姜伟雄

同济大学电子与信息工程学院助理教授

姜伟雄，同济大学电子与信息工程学院助理教授。2017 年获哈尔滨工业大学电磁场与无线技术专业学士学位，2022 年获中国科学院大学微电子学与固体电子学专业博士学位。

博士毕业后先后任职于华为海思图灵架构部与蔚来汽车自动驾驶芯片开发部，从事 GPU 与 NPU 的架构定义、关键模块论证、方案设计、RTL 开发及性能建模等工作，系统覆盖数字芯片前端核心岗位中的架构、建模与设计三大方向。

近五年来在 TCAD、TCAS-I、TCAS-II、TVLSI、DAC 等集成电路领域高水平期刊与会议上发表论文十余篇，其中以第一作者或通讯作者发表 6 篇，拥有国际授权专利 2 项。

Profile

Weixiong Jiang

Assistant Professor, College of Electronics and Information Engineering, Tongji University

Weixiong Jiang is an Assistant Professor at the College of Electronics and Information Engineering, Tongji University. He received his B.Eng. in Electromagnetic Field and Wireless Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2017 and his Ph.D. in Microelectronics and Solid State Electronics from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2022.

After completing his doctorate, he worked in Huawei HiSilicon's Turing Architecture Department and NIO's autonomous driving chip division, focusing on GPU and NPU architecture definition, key module analysis, solution design, RTL development, and performance modeling across the major front-end roles of digital chip design.

Over the past five years, he has published more than ten papers in leading integrated-circuit journals and conferences such as TCAD, TCAS-I, TCAS-II, TVLSI, and DAC, including six papers as first or corresponding author, and holds two granted international patents.

Mentoring Philosophy

  • Methodology first, with theory and practice reinforcing each other
    • Students first learn a general methodology that spans coding, paper writing, and technical proposal design, then internalize it through daily research work.
  • Industry-oriented training with full-stack capability
    • Research training covers the full LPU stack, including inference frameworks, operator design and optimization, architecture exploration, microarchitecture optimization, and software-hardware co-design.
    • Foundational skills include Verilog, C++, and UVM verification; higher-level skills emphasize abstraction, deduction, systems thinking, and structured communication.
  • Organized research with shared infrastructure
    • The group develops research around a shared LPU full-stack platform, with students jointly maintaining the platform and deeply optimizing key modules.
  • Progressive and personalized training
    • Debugging or UVM verification -> independent C++ or Verilog development -> small-module proposal writing -> medium-module proposal writing -> large-module proposal writing -> leadership of another core module.

Communication Mechanism

  • A full group meeting is held every week to align progress.
  • Additional technical meetings are organized as needed for proposal walkthroughs, design discussions, and code reviews.
  • One-on-one discussions are maintained to resolve issues in a timely manner.

Internships and Career Development

  • Career support
    • Includes resume revision and mock interviews.
    • Outstanding students may receive referrals to core teams in major companies.
  • Internship arrangement
    • The group maintains internship partnerships with two major chip companies, and strong students can be directly referred.
    • In principle, students are not encouraged to take outside internships before meeting graduation requirements; after those requirements are met, internship participation is fully supported.
