AI Processors and Software Stacks

Focused on edge LLM processors, operator optimization, architecture exploration, and software-hardware co-design.

Weixiong Jiang is an Assistant Professor at Tongji University. Before joining academia, he worked in Huawei HiSilicon's Turing Architecture Department and NIO's autonomous driving chip team on GPU/NPU architecture, solution design, RTL development, and performance modeling.

招生说明

欢迎有意报考的硕士、博士同学联系。课题组围绕 LPU 全栈开展有组织科研，强调方法论训练、系统能力培养与产业场景结合。

培养方式覆盖代码能力、系统设计、论文写作与工程落地，适合希望同时提升研究深度与产业能力的同学。

Research

  • End-to-end LPU design for edge large language models
  • Inference frameworks, operator design, and optimization
  • Architecture exploration and microarchitecture design
  • Software-hardware co-design

Recruitment

Prospective M.S. and Ph.D. students are welcome to reach out. The group is organized around a shared LPU full-stack research platform with strong emphasis on methodology, systems thinking, and industry relevance.

The training path covers coding, system design, paper writing, and engineering execution for students aiming to build both research depth and practical capability.
